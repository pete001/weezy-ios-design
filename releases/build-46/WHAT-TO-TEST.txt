Build 46 adds the final launch polish and gender-neutral sharing.

• Share a public wishlist. On a fresh install with notification permission not yet decided, the gift-alert choice appears before sharing. Turn on alerts or Share without alerts should both continue to the normal iPhone share sheet. Existing notification choices are respected.

• Use the phone in dark mode and at the largest text size. The app stays light; wishlist sharing, search and basket controls should stay readable and reachable.
• Gift alerts, VoiceOver, haptics, Shopify login/payment and verified order updates still need real-phone acceptance. No test purchase or notification has been sent by this release.
