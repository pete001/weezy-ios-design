When the explainer shows itself

I
Onboarding finishesSign in, sync, your name, purchases found. Nothing extra here.
II
Founding Collector badgeUnlock moment plays and is dismissed.

First Shop visit after thatSnap, Stack, Style (FF-06) opens once, 0.6s after the feed settles.

Never alongsideThe alerts prompt, a badge unlock, an unwrap, a sheet already open, or a deep link (shared wishlist, gift). If any of these happen, wait for the next Shop visit.

After it has shownOnly from Ways to wear. Never again automatically, even after reinstall sign-in.

Build. One flag (hasSeenSnapStackStyle) stored with the customer, not the device, so a new phone doesn't repeat it. Interruption queue: one celebration or prompt per screen appearance, badge first.
