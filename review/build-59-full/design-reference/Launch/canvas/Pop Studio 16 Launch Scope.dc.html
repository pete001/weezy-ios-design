SEE MY CHARMSShare the unwrap
Nothing manual. Unwrapping (1) reveals the exact variant with a sparkle burst, (2) adds it to My Charms as a received gift, (3) removes it from the wishlist, (4) bumps any set meter with a glow on the new segment. Ownership. The order belongs to the buyer’s Shopify account, so order-history sync never sees it. The webhook that sent the gift push stores an app-only "gift received" record on the recipient (variant, order ID, no buyer name or price); unwrap confirms it into My Charms. Share the unwrap opens the share sheet with a reveal card, the natural thank-you.
